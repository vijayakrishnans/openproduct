# topics-and-community (TC): risks and open questions

## Risks

**R1. Topic sprawl** (`nextjs`, `next-js`, `next.js`). Mitigation: normalising and aliases (D1), a page only from two pieces of writing, and moderator merge.

**R2. Gaming the rankings** with helpful-vote rings, or many accounts voting. Mitigation: one vote per account, votes only from builders with a profile, every formula shown, and moderators can clear a vote ring. No ranking pays out anything.

**R3. Search counts leaking who searched.** Mitigation: only a count per topic per day is stored (D5).

**R4. Spam in public sections.** Mitigation: the existing per-account limits, reports and moderation reach every new kind; launches must point at a build the author maintains.

**R5. Empty pages before people write.** Mitigation: demo data on stage and prod for every milestone, and topics seeded from build categories and stacks so pages have builds from day one.

## Open questions

**Q1. May anyone start a topic, or only pick from a list?** Default: anyone, by using it (D1); its page appears from two pieces of writing; moderators merge and hide.

**Q2. What's a "baseline" post?** Default: a starter template others copy, with a required repo link and "Use this baseline" to Orunbase. If baselines should instead be Orunbase's own baseline registry entries, TC2 links those instead.

**Q3. Should discussions from openproduct.live get topics?** Default: no; they're read-only here, and keep their tags.
